# Research → site viewer: candidates

Entries in `web/research/data.json` that have a place and a time and could appear in the viewer (`web/viewer.html`).
A list only, written 2026-10-12; nothing here is built yet. Ids link to the Research page (`research/list/#id`); each
entry's `viewer` field has the one-line idea, `place` the model id, `timeline` the period.

## 2. Periods for a timeline slider
- Bunker Hill: urban renewal adopted 1959-03-31 (bh-006); regrading, hilltop lowered about 30 ft (bh-008);
  Castle and Salt Box moved and burned 1969 (bh-009).
- Angels Flight (entity E005): opened 1901 at 3rd & Hill (af-001); dismantled 1969-05-18 → rebuilt on the north edge of
  Y-1 1996-02-24 (af-004, af-005); closed 2001-02-01 → 2010-03-15 (af-007); closed 2013-09-05 → 2017-08-31 (af-008).
- Angels Knoll: interim park from at least Dec 2001 (opening year unknown) to the fence of 2013-07-30 (park-001,
  park-004); still fenced; maintenance contract Oct 2025 → Jun 2027 (park-006).
- Disposition: option expired 2025-09-30; surplus declaration 2025-10-24; NOA not issued as of 2026-10-12
  (disp-001, disp-002, disp-003); settlement pending in CF 26-1436 (lit-004).
