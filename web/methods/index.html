2.4 Estimated facades

Airborne LiDAR sees roofs well and walls badly: glass curtain walls reflect the laser away and the near-vertical view grazes the walls. Points are added on the walls of each building's stepped volume, only where no measured return is near, mostly on the storey lines read from the building's own wall returns, and not in the lowest metres above the ground (arcades, entrances and plazas stay open). They thin with distance like the measured cloud. They are estimates, not measurements: the viewer shows them as their own layer, "Facades (estimated)". (site-model/houdini/tools/synth_facades.py)
